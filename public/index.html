🎵 PitchPop Music · For kids

Learn perfect pitch by colored chords

PitchPop started as a game we play at home: I play a chord on the piano, and my kids grab the matching color flag and shout it out. The app turns that into a quiz. Tap the rainbow to hear one of nine chords, pick its color from four choices, and get confetti when you're right. There's also a free-play mode for tapping any chord, plus a progress page for each child that tracks accuracy and which colors have come up. The piano sound is synthesized in the browser, so the app ships no audio files.

MathPop Math · For kids

Multiplication, addition, subtraction

MathPop is a sibling to PitchPop that uses arithmetic instead of chords. Kids pick multiplication, addition, subtraction, or a random mix, then solve five-problem rounds with big colored cards, chimes, and confetti. Each child gets a difficulty range that fits them. The wrong answers come from common slip-ups, like being off by one factor or adding instead of multiplying, so getting it right means knowing the answer rather than ruling out silly numbers. Progress is saved on the device and broken down by operation.

Kachun Quizer Study · Flashcards

Photo your notes, get quizzed on them

Take a photo of your notes or paste them in, and Kachun Quizer turns them into an Anki-style flashcard deck. Tesseract.js reads text from the photo, and a small NLP library picks the most important word in each sentence to blank out. Each deck uses spaced repetition, so facts you miss come back sooner and facts you know show up less often. There's no AI, account, or server: OCR runs in the browser and your decks and history stay on your device.

HomeDiary Home · Full-stack

Track your home's history and documents

HomeDiary is a private diary for your home. Sign in, add your properties, and log a timeline of everything that happens to them: purchase, damage, repairs, inspections, and more, each with photos and documents attached. Paperwork like purchase documents, insurance policies, and warranties gets its own place for each property. It's a full-stack app with a React and TypeScript front end, an Express and Prisma API on Postgres, and file storage on Cloudflare R2. It runs as a single Docker service on Render.
